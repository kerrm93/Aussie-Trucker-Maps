Network & route layers

GeoPackage (.gpkg) from the National Network Map, KML / GPX routes from the Route Planner, or GeoJSON. Files stay on this phone and work without signal.

Get network data (.gpkg)

  1. Open the NHVR National Network Map.
  2. Use View by → Networks and pick the state.
  3. Select the network for your vehicle (e.g. B-double, Road train, PBS) and tap the ? icon.
  4. Tap Download Network. A .gpkg file is saved to your downloads.
  5. Come back here, tap Import file and choose it. Set its role to Network.

Get a planned route (.kml / .gpx)

  1. Plan the trip in the NHVR Route Planner (Portal → Information Hub → Route Planner, no login needed).
  2. Use Download route and choose KML or GPX.
  3. Import it here. Its role becomes Route, and you get an off-route alert while driving.

Welcome

HV Network Driver uses your location while the app is open to:

  • show whether you're on a heavy vehicle network you've loaded
  • warn you if you leave your planned route
  • show your speed and position on the map

Your location stays on this phone. It isn't stored or sent to us. Only map images are downloaded for the area you're viewing.

This app isn't made by or affiliated with the NHVR. Always follow road signs, notices and permits.
