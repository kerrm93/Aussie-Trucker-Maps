HV Network Driver – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

HV Network Driver ("the app") is published by [YOUR NAME OR BUSINESS]. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Location

The app uses your device's precise location only while the app is open on screen. It uses it to show your position and speed on the map, to check whether you are on a heavy vehicle network you have loaded, and to warn you if you leave a route you have loaded. All of this processing happens on your device. Your location is not stored on our servers, sold or shared with us or anyone else. The app does not use location in the background.

Files you import

Network and route files you import (for example GeoPackage, KML or GPX files downloaded from the NHVR) are stored only on your device, so they work without mobile signal. You can delete them at any time in the app's Layers screen or by uninstalling the app.

Vehicle details and fatigue timer

Vehicle details and fatigue timer entries you type in are stored only on your device. They are not sent anywhere.

Map images

To draw the background street map, the app downloads map images from a map provider ([MapTiler AG / OpenStreetMap Foundation]). Like any web request, the provider receives your device's IP address and which map area is being viewed. They do not receive your GPS position from the app. See the provider's own privacy policy for how they handle these requests.

What we don't do

The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking SDKs. We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

Children

The app is intended for licensed heavy vehicle drivers and is not directed at children.

Not affiliated with government

HV Network Driver is an independent app. It is not made by, endorsed by or affiliated with the National Heavy Vehicle Regulator (NHVR) or any government agency. Network data comes from files you download yourself from the NHVR at maps.nhvr.gov.au.
